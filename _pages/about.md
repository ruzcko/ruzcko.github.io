I am a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence at [UNIST](https://www.unist.ac.kr/) and a member of the [IRIS Lab](https://irisunist.wixsite.com/irislab). My research applies machine learning and computer vision to remote sensing, Earth observation, and climate-related problems.

My current work focuses on building reliable AI methods for understanding changes in the Earth's surface from satellite imagery, including change detection and synthetic-data-assisted learning.

### Research interests

- Artificial intelligence for Earth observation
- Remote sensing and satellite image analysis
- Computer vision and change detection
- Climate and environmental applications
