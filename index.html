About

I am an AI researcher. What holds my attention is the methods themselves, and what breaks when you take them off a benchmark and point them at something real.

Right now that something is satellites — weather, climate, disasters. Before it was crops, and before that, cells. The domain keeps changing; the problems don't. Too few labels, distributions that drift, instruments that disagree. I follow those wherever they turn up next.

It’s a secret for now. 🤫

Research Interests

Mostly I chase methods: multimodal and vision–language models, generative and state space architectures, and the long-running problem of learning something useful when labels are scarce — synthetic data, transfer learning, super-resolution.

At the moment those get pointed at weather and climate: precipitation nowcasting, change detection, soil-moisture retrieval, damage assessment from satellite imagery. Ask again next year and the list will probably have moved.
